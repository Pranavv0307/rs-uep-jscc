# Insights

- Based on 3 channel seed(s); 5000 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.34.
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 1.26 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (two_tier_50_50@energy). This is the block-length effect of a single large RS block, which tiered coding gives up.
- Peak gain: +1.94 dB at erasure_rate = 0.4 by three_tier_25_50_25@energy (95% CI ±0.06, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: energy +1.40 dB, l1 +1.36 dB.
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.4: 5th-percentile PSNR 18.83 dB (three_tier_25_50_25@energy) vs 17.25 dB (uniform).
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR 16.49 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@energy) vs 15.90 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: std across channel seeds stays below 0.3 dB everywhere.
- `uniform` shows its steepest fall (-3.89 dB) between erasure_rate = 0.3 and 0.35 -- its cliff.
