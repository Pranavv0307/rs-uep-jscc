# Insights

- Based on 3 channel seed(s); 5000 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.32.
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 0.83 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (three_tier_25_50_25@predicted). This is the block-length effect of a single large RS block, which tiered coding gives up.
- Peak gain: +2.97 dB at erasure_rate = 0.4 by three_tier_25_50_25@predicted (95% CI ±0.14, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: predicted +2.30 dB, oracle +2.18 dB, random +0.51 dB, attention +0.29 dB.
- Share of the reference ranking's gain captured: predicted 106%, random 23%, attention 13%. A share above 100% means that source allocated protection better than the reference.
- Random-order control gains +0.51 dB versus oracle +2.18 dB, so the benefit comes from *which* channels are protected, not merely from tiering.
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.4: 5th-percentile PSNR 18.77 dB (three_tier_25_50_25@predicted) vs 16.63 dB (uniform).
- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR 15.86 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@predicted) vs 15.33 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: std across channel seeds stays below 0.3 dB everywhere.
- `uniform` shows its steepest fall (-4.25 dB) between erasure_rate = 0.3 and 0.35 -- its cliff.
