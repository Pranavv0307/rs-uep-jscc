- Based on 3 channel seed(s); 5000 images per seed. All gains below are paired per seed against the baseline (same packet losses), so they carry no luck-of-the-draw advantage.
- Break-even: the best protected method overtakes `uniform` from about erasure_rate = 0.32.
- Low-loss penalty: even the best method is 0.71 dB below `uniform` at erasure_rate = 0.3 (three_tier_25_50_25@energy). This is the block-length effect of a single large RS block, which tiered coding gives up.
- Peak gain: +2.49 dB at erasure_rate = 0.45 by three_tier_25_25_50@energy (95% CI ±0.05, significant).
- Importance sources ranked by their best scheme's mean gain over erasure_rate >= 0.35: energy +1.97 dB, oracle +1.03 dB.
- Share of the reference ranking's gain captured: energy 191%. A share above 100% means that source allocated protection better than the reference.

- Worst-case images at erasure_rate = 0.5: 5th-percentile PSNR 17.48 dB (three_tier_25_25_50@energy) vs 16.90 dB (uniform).
- Seed sensitivity: std across channel seeds stays below 0.3 dB everywhere.
